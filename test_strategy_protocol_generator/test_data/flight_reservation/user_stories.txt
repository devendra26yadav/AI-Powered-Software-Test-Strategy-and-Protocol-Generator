Northline Reservations release 2.4 user stories

US-01 Hold a seat before payment
As a traveler, I want the fare I selected to be held while I pay, so that the seat is not sold to someone else during checkout.
Acceptance: creating a pending booking reduces sellable seats by the seats in that booking (FR-002). The hold lasts 15 minutes (BR-001).

US-02 Pay the amount I was shown
As a traveler, I want to be charged the total displayed when I held the fare, so that the price does not change at payment.
Acceptance: the captured amount equals the held total (FR-003). A successful capture confirms the booking and sends one email (FR-004).

US-03 Recover cleanly when my card is declined
As a traveler, I want a declined card to cancel my unpaid booking and put the seats back on sale, so that I am not charged and the airline does not keep an unpaid hold.
Acceptance: payment failure cancels the pending booking, restores only that booking's seats, and does not send a confirmation email (FR-005, FR-006, BR-003, BR-004). The traveler can search again and cannot pay the cancelled reference (FR-009).

US-04 Do not undo the cleanup twice
As an airline inventory owner, I want a repeated failure notice to be ignored after the first cleanup, so that a duplicate event does not create an extra seat.
Acceptance: delivering the same PaymentFailed event twice leaves the booking cancelled once and does not increase sellable seats a second time (FR-007). An event for an unknown booking changes nothing (FR-008).

US-05 Keep a record of seat changes
As a support agent, I want each capacity change tied to a booking and an event, so that I can explain why a seat count moved.
Acceptance: each change stores booking reference, event identifier, seat count, and UTC time (FR-010).

US-06 Leave a paid booking alone
As a traveler who already received a confirmation, I want a late failure notice not to cancel my ticket.
Acceptance: a PaymentFailed event after confirmation is quarantined and the booking stays confirmed (BR-005).
